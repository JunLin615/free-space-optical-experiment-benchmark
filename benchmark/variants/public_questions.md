## VAR-SEED-1-1-P-c8828a9799d3

Parent: SEED-1-1 revision 0.1.5.

A collimated laser beam is incident nearly normally on an adjustable plane mirror. The mirror rotates 0.75° about a vertical axis. A viewing screen is normal to the nominal reflected ray, 2.8 m from the mirror along that ray. Neglect higher-order corrections associated with the initial angle of incidence. Report the angle change and spot displacement as magnitudes.

q1. Find the change in direction of the reflected beam.
q2. Estimate the transverse displacement of the spot on the screen.
q3. Identify the most direct geometric quantity to change if the displacement must fall to one quarter of its original value.
