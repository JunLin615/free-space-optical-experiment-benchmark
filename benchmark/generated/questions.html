Free-Space Optical Experimental Reasoning — Question Bank

Question bank generated from canonical case files.

SEED-1-1 — Mirror rotation and far-field displacement

A collimated laser beam is incident nearly normally on an adjustable plane mirror. The mirror rotates 0.50° about a vertical axis. A viewing screen is normal to the nominal reflected ray, 2.0 m from the mirror along that ray. Neglect higher-order corrections associated with the initial angle of incidence. Report the angle change and spot displacement as magnitudes.

Given quantities

  • mirror rotation = 0.5 deg
  • screen distance = 2.0 m

Questions

  1. Find the change in direction of the reflected beam.
  2. Estimate the transverse displacement of the spot on the screen.
  3. Identify the most direct geometric quantity to change if the displacement must fall to one quarter of its original value.

SEED-1-2 — Establishing an axis with two steering mirrors and two apertures

A collimated laser must propagate at a specified height and direction. Two adjustable steering mirrors and two well-separated apertures are available. The aperture centers define the target output line and are reachable with the available mirror travel.

Questions

  1. Describe a stable procedure for aligning the beam through both aperture centers.
  2. Explain why centering only at the distant aperture does not establish the desired line.
  3. Give a measurable convergence check for the alignment.
