# Spatial-filter alignment failure

When a focused free-space beam misses a spatial-filter pinhole, first reduce
power, place a viewing card at the focus, and adjust transverse pinhole position
with small steps. Distinguish clipping from a focus displaced along the optical
axis: transverse motion changes the bright spot overlap, whereas axial motion
changes the spot size at the aperture. Do not infer that every low-throughput
condition calls for a smaller aperture.
