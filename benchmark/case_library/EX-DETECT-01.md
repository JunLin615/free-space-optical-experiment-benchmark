# Detector linearity calibration

To test whether a photodiode response is linear, attenuate a stable free-space
beam with calibrated neutral-density filters and record dark-subtracted output.
For an illustrative filter transmitting 12% of incident power, a linear response
should fall to about 12% of its previous dark-subtracted reading. If the ratio
changes with incident power, inspect amplifier saturation, offset, and detector
bandwidth before interpreting the optical signal.

Record the filter transmission and dark offsets in the measurement log.
