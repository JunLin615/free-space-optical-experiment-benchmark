# Deterministic-ready vNext audit

This audit covers the five deterministic-ready cases that were not release-verified in `0.1.0-rc1`. It does not promote case status. The original canonical cases, rc1 evaluator, and historical run artifacts remain unchanged. Versioned case contracts are under `benchmark/cases_vnext/`; independent challenge calculations are in `tests/test_vnext_deterministic_evidence.py`.

| Case | Independent physical check | vNext contract or remaining blocker | Recommendation for scientific review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEED-2-1 | Gaussian Fourier/q-parameter waist is about 16.93 µm; doubling lens-plane radius halves ideal waist. Mutated focal length invalidates old gold. | Revision 0.3.0 separates numerical q1 from the offered order-scale q2. The 20% q1 reporting band admits 20 µm to one significant figure but rejects a bare 10 µm scale label in q1. | Eligible for release challenge after the versioned fixture runner checks all existing positive and adversarial examples against revision 0.3.0. The old `alternative_valid_order_scale` example must be reclassified for q1; q2 still accepts 10 µm. |
| SEED-2-4 | Gaussian envelope gives Rayleigh range ratio 4 and divergence ratio 1/2. A 3× waist mutation gives 9 and 1/3 and invalidates old gold. | q3 requests an application-specific disadvantage. The current enumerated cost set abstains on unfamiliar but plausible physical costs. Merely adding synonyms would not prove full task coverage. | Keep executable, not release-verified, until a bounded application context or a qualified, alternative-tolerant constraint oracle is available. |
| SEED-3-1 | Ray-transfer focus condition yields image distance 2f, signed magnification −1, and real inverted image. A 3f object mutation yields 1.5f and −0.5. | Revision 0.3.0 explicitly bounds the neutral JSON claims `image_type` and `orientation` to physical enums. Explanatory prose can use equivalent wording. | Eligible for review with the versioned scorer and 12 local challenge fixtures. |
| SEED-3-7 | First Airy zero gives 0.61λ/NA = 0.671 µm; at fixed wavelength larger NA narrows depth of focus. Mutating to 600 nm and NA 0.4 yields 0.915 µm. | Revision 0.3.0 uses the neutral categorical `depth_of_focus_trend` with three possible directions. Unit alternatives in q1 are accepted. | Eligible for review with the versioned scorer and 12 local challenge fixtures. |
| SEED-5-5 | Square-law intensity of two overlapping fields contains a difference-frequency cross term; an ideal orthogonal inner product removes it. Offset mutation to 125 MHz invalidates old gold. | Revision 0.3.0 uses a dimensionless response-order quantity and a neutral component-source field for q2, and retains the explicit no-projector q3 condition. | Eligible for review with the versioned scorer and 12 local challenge fixtures. |

The vNext typed JSON fields make the machine answer format finite; human explanations remain optional and may use equivalent terminology. The scorer evaluates the typed physical claims and does not assign extra credit for prose. This is a contract clarification, so the 0.3.0 copies cannot be substituted into rc1 or used to rescore the frozen baseline. A final release decision also needs integrated vNext-runtime execution, fixture-tool execution, release asset pinning, and coordinator review.
