# Project status after the v0.2.0 freeze

The initial public scored release is **v0.2.0**, prepared for the integrator to merge, tag, and publish. Its immutable manifest contains nine `release_verified` cases. The canonical case bank contains 64 English scientific identities with matching English and Chinese public question views. The original Chinese seed HTML is preserved as provenance. Qualification and normal scoring are autonomous.

The earlier `0.1.0-rc1` and `0.2.0-rc1` releases remain historical lines. The measured three-model campaign evaluates `0.2.0-rc1`, including its documented `SEED-3-1` contract behavior. It is retained for reproducibility and descriptive research; it is not a stable-release model ranking.

Normal development is frozen at this release boundary. Corrections to scientific contracts, new scored cases, broader semantic scoring, or new evaluation integrations require versioned future work and independent evidence. Maintenance may clarify documentation and repair infrastructure while preserving every historical release and run commitment.

[Issue #3](https://github.com/JunLin615/free-space-optical-experiment-benchmark/issues/3) remains open: development scorers for open designs and diagnoses are conservative, and unfamiliar plausible answers may be unresolved. Additional scientific cases and stronger open-reasoning coverage are optional research directions, not prerequisites for v0.2.0. [Issue #14](https://github.com/JunLin615/free-space-optical-experiment-benchmark/issues/14) documents the rc1 `SEED-3-1` ambiguity addressed in the stable versioned contract.

The [historical roadmap](project_roadmap.md) records the original research plan. The [release notes](release_v0.2.0.md) and this status document describe the current release boundary.
